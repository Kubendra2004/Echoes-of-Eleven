# 🔍 Echoes of Eleven: The Burari Case

A psychological thriller detective game based on India's most disturbing unsolved crime: the Burari Deaths of 2007.

## About the Game

**Genre:** Story-Driven Detective Thriller  
**Platform:** Windows, macOS, Linux  
**Engine:** Godot 4.3 (Forward+)  
**Status:** Early Access  

### Story

On July 31, 2007, investigators discovered eleven members of the Burari family hanging by cloth in their upstairs room in New Delhi. The scene was ritualistic, deliberate, and utterly unexplainable. As Detective Chen, you arrive at the crime scene to uncover the truth.

## Features & Recent Updates

### 🕵️ Modern Investigation Mechanics
- **Dynamic Flashlight:** Press `F` to toggle your flashlight and explore the highly atmospheric, dark environment clearly.
- **Fluid Movement:** Fully smooth camera interpolation and procedural head-bobbing for maximum immersion.
- **Smooth UI:** "Examine" prompts gracefully fade in and out as you look at evidence.

### 🧩 Deduction & Dialogue
- Examine 15+ pieces of evidence and connect them on your Evidence Board.
- Branching dialogue with 50+ paths affecting your Reputation and Stress levels.

### 💾 Optimized Save System
- **Space-Saver Save Files:** Save files are now hyper-compressed JSON, saving disk space.
- Save your game directly from the **Pause Menu** (Press Shift+Enter to pause).

### ⚡ Performance & Resources
- **Optimized for Low VRAM:** Unnecessary heavy post-processing like SDFGI has been disabled to prioritize high FPS while retaining visual fidelity.
- **Procedural PBR Textures:** The game uses zero heavy external texture images. Instead, all wood floors, walls, and metals use Godot's procedural Noise mapping, drastically reducing the total game size.
